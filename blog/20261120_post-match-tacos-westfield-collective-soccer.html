Post-Match Tacos at Westfield Collective During This Weekend's Soccer Showcase

The November 20–22 women’s college soccer showcase brings teams, coaches, and families to Westfield, just one mile north of Westfield Collective.

After hours on the fields or between showcase matches, stop by Taco Heist inside Westfield Collective (750 E. 181st St.) for warm double corn tortillas, marinated street tacos, loaded bowls, and house-made salsas—with a menu that is 100% gluten-free and nut-free.

Weekend Hours at Westfield Collective

  • Friday: 11:00 AM – 10:00 PM
  • Saturday: 10:00 AM – 10:00 PM
  • Sunday: 10:00 AM – 9:00 PM

Taco Heist is an independent restaurant concept at Westfield Collective and is not affiliated with or sponsored by Droplight Grand Park Sports Campus or the showcase organizers.
